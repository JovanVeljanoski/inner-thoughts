01 — The threshold

The dark is not
empty.

It is crowded with all the things we were too bright to notice: the patient pulse under stone, the soft animal inside the machine, the old moon turning its blind face toward us.

Come closer. Not to conquer it. To be witnessed by it.

02 — A small confession
I wanted to build
somewhere the night
would love you back.

Not a temple. Not a warning. Just a room with no ceiling, where every lost thing can become luminous for a moment.

There is no map for this place.
There is only the next honest step.
