Field notes / 000

The dark is not
a void.

It is a living archive. A soft animal with its ribs spread wide enough to hold every forgotten thing.

We call it empty because we are frightened by what it contains: the first thought, the last light, the version of ourselves that existed before we learned to look away.

→Do not mistake the absence of an answer for an absence of a witness. Some things listen in frequencies we have not yet earned.
